Music

John Moose Band

Alternative rock / folk · Sweden

With my freinds in cultural cooperative John Moose/Anlesser—focusing we make music as well as audiovisual and digital productions. We have gained recognition both nationally and internationally, receiving awards such as the Guldägget (Golden Egg) and the Guldvargen (Golden Wolf). We were also honored with the title “Värmland Cooperative of the Year 2016.”

Music and Digital Media

John Moose: Go to the The Forest App 2015

Location-based music app

We released our debut album through an app that made the music available only when the listener was in a forest. It used the phone’s location to connect the listening experience to the album’s story about leaving society for nature.
