Music

John Moose Band

Alternative rock / folk · Sweden

With my friends in cultural cooperative John Moose/Anlesser we make music as well as audiovisual and digital productions. We have gained recognition both nationally and internationally, receiving awards such as the Guldägget (Golden Egg) and the Guldvargen (Golden Wolf). We were also honored with the title "Värmland Cooperative of the Year 2016."

Digital Media

John Moose: Go to the Forest App 2015

Location-based music app

We released our debut album through an app that made the music available only when the listener was in a forest. It used the phone's location to connect the listening experience to the album's story about leaving society for nature.

Read about it:

  • TIME — This Swedish Band Released Music You Can Only Hear in the Woods →
  • Rolling Stone — Swedish Band Make Album Available Exclusively in the Woods →
John Moose: O VR experience

Music and virtual reality app

For our EP O, we developed a second mobile experience using virtual reality. Where the first app asked listeners to go into the forest, this one brought a forest experience to them.
